The Starter Template Repository contains ready-made campaign content. Each item you add becomes an independent resource that you can edit and use in campaigns.

Catalog access requires permission to modify system settings, normally available to administrators. If you cannot open it, ask an administrator to add the content you need. Once added, the resources are shared according to the usual resource permissions.

## Choose and add content

1. Open **Browse starter templates** from the email-template, landing-page, or learning-page list.
2. Choose the content type and inspect the available entries.
3. Select the entries you want and review their previews.
4. Where available, choose email prefill or company-logo options.
5. Add the selected content.
6. Open the newly created resources, customize them, and save your changes.

Choose matching email and landing-page content when both are available. They are added separately, so check that names, document titles, and other details agree after customization.

If an addition is interrupted, inspect your resource lists before adding the same entries again. Some items may already have been saved.

## Customize the copies

Adding an entry never overwrites an existing resource. Repeated additions get distinct names, and you can rename them afterward.

Changes in the catalog do not update your copies. Editing one copied resource also does not update the other resources you added alongside it.

For supported landing pages, email prefill inserts recipient personalization. Company-logo options embed the selected image into that copy. Changing the image later does not replace it in resources already created.

Learning pages support static educational content and do not personalize recipient details. Adapt their reporting instructions to your organization's process.

## Check the complete experience

Preview the content, then send a test email and try a controlled campaign. Check the message, page, submission behavior, and learning destination together.

Interactive sign-in examples do not validate credentials or retrieve files from the service they resemble. Intermediate steps do not necessarily record a submission; the final action uses Ruphish's presence-only tracking.

Continue with [Email templates](resources/email-templates.md), [Landing pages](resources/landing-pages.md), or [Learning pages](resources/learning-pages.md) for authoring and testing.
