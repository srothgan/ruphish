Use this section when you look after the people and policies in your Ruphish installation. Sign in with an **Administrator** account to manage users and company settings.

| Task | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Understand what colleagues can see and change | [Shared access and permissions](shared-access-and-permissions.md) |
| Give someone access, reset a password, or remove an account | [Manage accounts](manage-accounts.md) |
| Require domain verification or enable reporting mailboxes and webhooks | [Company settings](company-settings.md) |
| Stop all active and scheduled campaigns | [Close active campaigns](close-active-campaigns.md) |

Each installation is a shared workspace. Creating separate accounts gives people their own sign-in and records who creates or edits resources; it does not separate their campaigns or recipients into private workspaces.

Server addresses, HTTPS certificates, database connections, and backups belong to [Hosting and maintenance](../hosting-and-maintenance/index.md). Changes to those settings usually require the person hosting Ruphish.
